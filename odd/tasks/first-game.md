## Specs
S1. "Create an original game inspired by Football Legends, not a copy of its characters, branding, artwork, audio or code." "Exactly two human players, one controlled by each phone." "Side-view football pitch with two goals." "Cartoon-style characters and ball physics." "Controls: left, right, jump and kick."
S2. "Two-minute matches; the higher score wins." User selected: "Empate y revancha opcional (recomendado)"; option description: "El partido termina empatado; ambos pueden aceptar otra partida."
S3. "QR connection screen." "Both players confirm readiness before starting." "Rematch flow."
S4. "Pause when a controller disconnects." User selected: "Esperar 60 s y cancelar sin ganador (recomendado)"; option description: "Si vuelve dentro del plazo, ambos confirman para reanudar. Si no, cancelamos." Question accepted: "Pausamos el partido y el reloj, reservando el lugar al mismo teléfono y navegador. ¿Qué pasa si no vuelve?"
S5. "The TV owns the authoritative game simulation: physics, collisions, goals, clock and score. Phones send control inputs. The VPS manages rooms and relays inputs; it must not simulate the match or stream video." "Keep game logic independent of the transport." "Do not include WebRTC in the initial scope. Its availability in an ordinary installed webOS app is not established."
S6. "No accounts, tournaments, special powers, online matchmaking or AI opponents in the first version." "Protect room ownership and player assignments: knowing a room code must not allow a third device to replace an existing player or impersonate the TV. Validate messages and bound their size and frequency."
S7. "Support simultaneous touch inputs, including movement plus an action." "Handle release, touch cancellation, backgrounding and disconnection without leaving controls stuck." "Provide automated checks for game rules, player isolation, room capacity, input handling and disconnections, plus a real-device test checklist."
S8. "Communicate with me in Spanish. Explain technical decisions clearly; do not assume I know networking or webOS development. Use English for code and identifiers." "Do not request secrets in chat, commit credentials, modify my VPS, publish services or open firewall ports without explicit approval." "No computer or additional home server may be required during gameplay."
S9. "pero no veo como conectarme para jugar, y haz toda la información en español". User-facing TV/phone text and usage guides must be Spanish; code/identifiers/protocol tokens stay English. Reproduce the pictured missing-room startup before fixing; preserve room ownership and actual background termination.
